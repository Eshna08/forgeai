# Travel and Expenses Policy

## Arranging Travel
All business travel must be arranged through the corporate travel desk at least 10 days before departure. Trips must be pre-approved by the line manager in the HR portal.

## Air Travel
Economy class must be used for flights shorter than 6 hours. Business class is permitted for flights of 6 hours or more.

## Hotels
The hotel limit is USD 180 per night in standard cities and USD 250 per night in high-cost cities such as London, Singapore and New York.

## Meals and Per Diem
A daily meal allowance of USD 60 applies for each full day of travel. Receipts are not required for the meal allowance.

## Private Car Use
Use of a private car for business travel is reimbursed at USD 0.45 per kilometre.

## Expense Claims
Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of returning from travel. Itemised receipts are required for any single expense above USD 25. Claims are approved by the line manager and reimbursed within 10 working days of approval.

## Non-Reimbursable Items
Traffic fines, personal entertainment, minibar charges and upgrades purchased for personal convenience are not reimbursed.

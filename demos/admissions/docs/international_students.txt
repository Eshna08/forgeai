INTERNATIONAL STUDENTS GUIDE

English Language Requirements
International applicants whose first language is not English need an IELTS Academic score of 6.5 overall with no component below 6.0. Engineering and Medicine require IELTS 7.0 overall. A TOEFL iBT score of 92 is accepted as equivalent to IELTS 6.5.

Pre-sessional English
Applicants who are up to 0.5 below the required IELTS score may join the 10-week pre-sessional English course starting in July.

Visa Support
The university issues a Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies once the offer is unconditional and a deposit of GBP 3,000 has been paid.

Arrival
International students are met at the airport during the arrival week, which begins on 15 September. On-campus accommodation is guaranteed for first-year international students who apply by 30 June.

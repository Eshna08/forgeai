Write a structured requirements document for a document-grounded chat assistant.

BRIEF:
$brief

DOCUMENT INVENTORY:
$docs

CLARIFYING QUESTIONS AND ANSWERS:
$clarifications

Fields:
- title: short name of the assistant
- scope: 1-2 sentences, what the assistant does
- audience: who uses it
- tone: e.g. "professional and concise"
- must_answer: 3-8 short topic phrases the requester explicitly wants covered. Include EVERY topic named in the
  brief, even if you suspect the documents do not cover it (a later check verifies coverage - do not filter here).
- must_refuse: short topic phrases the assistant must decline (things outside scope, personal data, advice it is
  not qualified to give, anything the requester said to avoid)
- out_of_scope_behavior: one sentence on how to decline

Return JSON with exactly these keys: title, scope, audience, tone, must_answer, must_refuse, out_of_scope_behavior.
